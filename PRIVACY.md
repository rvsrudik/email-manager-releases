# Email Manager privacy policy

Email Manager is a Mac app that runs on your own computer.

- **Your data stays on your Mac.** Mail, Google Drive files, calendar events, recordings and everything the app indexes are stored only in a database on your Mac. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server of ours; there is none.
- **Google data.** With your permission the app reads your Gmail (over IMAP) and Google Drive, and changes them only when you approve a change in the app. Google user data is used only to show, search and organise it for you on your Mac, never for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to anyone else.
- **Claude.** When you ask Claude a question in the app, the question and the items Claude looks up go to Anthropic through your own Claude account, under Anthropic's terms.
- **Removing access.** Remove an account in the app, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder (~/Library/Application Support/email-manager) removes everything it stored.
